Kaeri   Lantern Street Notes
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    The lamp room sat at the top of the stairs, past the
  door that stuck in wet weather. Mio had learned to lift
  the handle before she turned it, the way her aunt did,
  and the door gave with a sigh that sounded almost
  polite.
    Inside, the shelves held every lantern the street had
  ever sent back for repair. Some were labelled in her
  aunt's narrow hand. Others had only a date and a house
  number, as if the lamp itself would explain the rest
  once it was lit.
    "You don't have to fix them all tonight," her aunt
  said from the doorway.
    "I know." Mio set the first one on the bench. Its
  glass was clouded, and a crack ran from the base to the
  hook like a river on a map. "I want to see which ones
  still remember how."
