Finish signing in to AniList in Kaeri.

This page should have opened Kaeri on your Android device. If you can read it, Android hasn’t linked kaeri.app to the app yet.

If it still doesn’t open

  1. Open Android’s Settings › Apps › Kaeri › Open by default.
  2. Turn on Open supported links and allow kaeri.app.
  3. Go back to Kaeri and sign in to AniList again.

Nothing from this page is sent anywhere. Your AniList sign-in stays in this browser tab, and closing it discards it.
