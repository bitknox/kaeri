Kaeri Cast receiver: audio decoders (pcm/*.js and pcm/*.wasm)

These files are FFmpeg's libavcodec and libavutil, version 7.1.1, compiled to WebAssembly with
Emscripten, together with a small wrapper (decoder.c, MIT). FFmpeg is licensed under the GNU
Lesser General Public License version 2.1 or later; the text is in COPYING.LGPLv2.1 beside this
file. The build is configured with --disable-gpl and --disable-nonfree.

They are separate files that the receiver loads at run time, only when a stream's audio needs
them, and they can be replaced with any build that exports the same functions.

FFmpeg source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.1.tar.xz
