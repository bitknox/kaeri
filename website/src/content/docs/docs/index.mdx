## The apps

Each app is native to its platform: SwiftUI on Apple devices, Jetpack Compose on Android, WinUI 3
on Windows and GTK on Linux, all on the same Rust engine. They have mpv built in and install from
the releases on GitHub, as [installation](/docs/getting-started/installation/#the-apps) describes.
On a Mac, the app and the terminal app share one history and one config.

The Android TV app is made for the remote's arrow keys. The Mac, iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows and
Linux apps can send an episode to it and act as its remote. The [features page](/features/#platforms)
shows what each app does.

## Reading

The apps on Mac, iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows and Linux read manga, manhwa and light novels too.
[Adding books](/docs/reading/adding-books/) covers your folders, servers and book plugins,
[the readers](/docs/reading/readers/) their settings and keys, and
[E-ink and e-readers](/docs/reading/e-ink/) Android e-ink tablets, Send to e-reader and the OPDS
feed. The terminal app finds books and opens them in the other apps, and the TV app is for
watching only.

## The terminal app

- Browsing, search and seasonal lists from AniList, with no account. `Ctrl-F` filters a search by
  genre, year, season, format, status and sort.
- A half-finished episode stays on the home screen with its position. `kaeri --continue` resumes it
  from the shell.
- [Playback](/docs/guides/player/) runs in mpv with Kaeri's own controls, seek bar thumbnails, a skip
  button, Up next and menus.
- Tracker sync covers AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, Simkl and Trakt. Changes made offline sync when
  you reconnect.
- Plugins are sandboxed WebAssembly components. A [plugin store](/docs/plugins/stores/) that someone
  you know runs lets you install their plugins and share your own.

[Keys and command line](/docs/guides/keybindings-cli/) lists the rest.

## In development

- More of the [plugin interface](/docs/plugins/authoring/), for the people who write plugins.
- Licensed services open in their own apps. Kaeri doesn't get round DRM, so a plugin can return a
  link that opens in that service instead of a stream for mpv.
