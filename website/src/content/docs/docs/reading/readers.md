---
title: The readers
description: The paged, long-strip and novel readers, their settings, Paper mode, chapter downloads and the keys on a Mac.
---

Kaeri has three readers: one that turns pages, one that scrolls a long strip, and one for light
novels. Each series opens in the reader that suits it, and you can change it.

## Choosing a reader

**Reader defaults** in Settings sets how each kind of book opens:

- Manga open as **Pages, right to left**, unless you pick **Pages, left to right** or **Long
  strip**.
- Webtoons always open as a long strip.
- Light novels open as **Pages** or **Scrolling**.

A source or the catalogue can say how a series reads, so a series from Korea, China or Taiwan opens
as a long strip. When Kaeri has to guess, the reader says so, as in "Looks like right to left".
Change the reader inside a series and that series keeps your choice on all your devices.

## Pages and the long strip

**Page layout** is Auto, One or Two. On Auto, an iPad in landscape or a wide Mac
window shows two pages side by side. **Tap zones** turn the page from the screen's edges,
from either half, or not at all.

## Light novels

The novel reader reflows the text to these settings:

- **Font**, Serif or Sans.
- **Text size**.
- **Line spacing**, Tight, Normal or Loose.
- **Margins**.
- **Justify text**.

**Look** is System, Paper, Light, Sepia or Dark.

## Paper mode

On a phone or tablet, **Paper mode** turns pages at once and keeps everything in the reader still.
On Android it can also turn pages with the volume keys, show pages in greys and quiet the phone
while you read. On an e-ink tablet the reader works this way from the start, as
[E-ink and e-readers](/docs/reading/e-ink/) describes.

## The end of a chapter

At the last page, Kaeri counts the chapter as read, says which trackers it updated and offers
**Next**. **Counts as read** in Settings decides when that happens: **After the last page**,
**Moving on from it** or **When you mark it**. **Send progress after** decides when the trackers
hear of it: when **A chapter is read**, or on **Closing the reader**.

Your page syncs to your other devices, and the reader there offers to go to where you stopped.

## Downloads

On a series page, **Download** keeps the next few unread chapters, **Every unread chapter** or the
ones you choose, and **Download new** in Updates keeps the new ones. On an iPhone, iPad or Android
device, **Chapter downloads on mobile data** decides whether downloads wait for Wi-Fi. **Delete
chapters after reading** removes a chapter once it's read, apart from those you bookmarked.

## Keys on a Mac

- The arrow keys turn the page, in the reading direction.
- Space and Shift-Space, or Page Up and Page Down, turn it too.
- Home and End go to the first and last page.
- `[` and `]` go to the previous and next chapter, and `G` goes to a page.
- ⌘L shows the chapters and ⌘T the reader settings.
- ⌘+, ⌘− and ⌘0 zoom.
- Esc closes the reader.

The Windows app has the same kind of keys, and F11 for full screen.
